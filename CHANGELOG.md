# Changelog

## v0.3.0 - 2026-10-17

### Changed

- Vehicles are drawn about twice as large, close to the size of sl's train.
- Trucks and construction machines puff smoke, and crawler tracks show spinning rollers.

## v0.2.0 - 2026-10-17

### Added

- `--loop` keeps driving vehicles until interrupted. Random vehicles never repeat back to back; with `--kind`, the same vehicle drives again and again.

## v0.1.1 - 2026-10-17

### Fixed

- A vehicle drives in from the right edge. It was drawn from the left edge and stood still until it reached it.

## v0.1.0 - 2026-10-17

Initial release.
